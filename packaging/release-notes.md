**Medical Paperwork Translator**: turns medical papers and bills into plain language, reads them aloud, checks bills for mistakes, scans papers with your camera, and adds dates to your calendar app.

### Mac (Apple silicon)
1. Download **Medical-Paperwork-Translator-Mac.zip** and double-click it to unzip.
2. Drag **Medical Paperwork Translator** into your **Applications** folder and double-click it.
3. The app isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time macOS will say it can't check it. Click **Done**, then open
   **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, click **Open Anyway**, and confirm.

### Windows
1. Download **Medical-Paperwork-Translator-Windows.zip**, right-click it, and choose **Extract All**.
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click **Medical Paperwork Translator.exe**.
3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

The three sample papers work right away. To translate your own papers, open **Translation service (API key)** at the top
of the app and paste an Anthropic API key. It is saved only on your computer.
